# Refund Policy — Basic Plan

This policy governs refunds on invoices for customers on the **Basic** plan.
It applies only to Basic customers. Premium plan refunds are governed by a
separate policy with a higher limit and additional eligible reasons.

## Per-request refund cap

A support agent may approve a refund of up to **$10.00 USD per request** on a
Basic invoice without escalation. Requests above $10.00 must be escalated to a
human billing specialist.

The cap applies per request, not per invoice. Multiple separate requests
against the same invoice are each subject to the cap, and are additionally
limited by the remaining refundable balance.

## Remaining refundable balance

A refund may never exceed the invoice's remaining refundable balance, which is
the original invoice amount minus any amount already refunded. A request
exceeding the remaining balance is declined, and the customer is told the
remaining balance.

## Eligible refund reasons

A Basic refund requires one of the following reasons:

- **Billing error** — the customer was charged an incorrect amount.
- **Duplicate charge** — the same service period was charged more than once.

Service outage credits are **not** included on the Basic plan. Outage-related
requests on a Basic plan are declined with an explanation, and the customer may
be offered a plan upgrade. Goodwill adjustments are not available on Basic.

## Time limit and its exceptions

A refund request must be made within **30 days** of the invoice issue date.

The single exception, which remains within the per-request cap:

- Confirmed duplicate charges may be refunded regardless of invoice age.

Billing errors originating in our own systems do not extend the 30-day limit on
the Basic plan; such cases are escalated instead.

## Contract requirement

Refunds require an **active** contract. Where a contract is inactive,
cancelled or suspended, no financial adjustment may be made, and the request is
handled under the contract eligibility policy instead.

## Currency

Refunds are issued in the original invoice currency. Cross-currency refunds are
not supported and must be escalated.
