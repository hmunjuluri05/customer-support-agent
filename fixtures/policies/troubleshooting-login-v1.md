# Troubleshooting — Sign-in Problems

Applies to all plans. No contract or tier requirement; this guidance is
available to any customer.

## Password reset

Direct the customer to the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page. The
reset link is valid for 30 minutes and may be requested up to five times per
hour.

## Account lockout

Ten consecutive failed sign-in attempts lock an account for 15 minutes. The
lock clears automatically. Support cannot shorten it.

## Multi-factor authentication

Where a customer has lost their authenticator device, recovery requires
identity verification by a human agent. Do not attempt to disable
multi-factor authentication through automated support; escalate instead.

## Single sign-on

For accounts using single sign-on, password resets are handled by the
customer's own identity provider, not by us.
