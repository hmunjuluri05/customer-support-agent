# Troubleshooting — Data Sync Delays

Applies to all plans.

## Expected sync interval

Data synchronises every 15 minutes under normal conditions. Delays of up to one
hour during peak periods are expected and are not a fault.

## When a delay exceeds four hours

Collect the workspace identifier and the timestamp of the last successful sync,
then escalate to engineering. Do not promise a resolution time.

## Manual resync

A customer may trigger one manual resync per hour from workspace settings.
Repeated manual resyncs do not accelerate a queued job.

## Not a confirmed incident

A delay on its own is not a confirmed incident. Incident confirmation comes
from engineering, and any entitlement that follows is governed by the
customer's own plan policy.
