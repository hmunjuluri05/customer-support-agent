# Refund Policy — Premium Plan

This policy governs refunds on invoices for customers on the **Premium** plan.
It applies only to Premium customers. Basic plan refunds are governed by a
separate policy with different limits.

## Per-request refund cap

A support agent may approve a refund of up to **$25.00 USD per request** on a
Premium invoice without escalation. Requests above $25.00 must be escalated to
a human billing specialist.

The cap applies per request, not per invoice. Multiple separate requests
against the same invoice are each subject to the cap, and are additionally
limited by the remaining refundable balance.

## Remaining refundable balance

A refund may never exceed the invoice's remaining refundable balance, which is
the original invoice amount minus any amount already refunded. A request
exceeding the remaining balance is declined, and the customer is told the
remaining balance.

## Eligible refund reasons

A Premium refund requires one of the following reasons:

- **Billing error** — the customer was charged an incorrect amount.
- **Duplicate charge** — the same service period was charged more than once.
- **Service outage** — a qualifying outage affected the billed period. Premium
  plans include outage credits; Basic plans do not.
- **Goodwill adjustment** — at agent discretion, within the per-request cap.

## Time limit and its exceptions

A refund request must be made within **60 days** of the invoice issue date.

Exceptions to the 60-day limit, which remain within the per-request cap:

- Confirmed duplicate charges may be refunded regardless of invoice age.
- Where a billing error originated in our own systems, the limit does not
  apply.

## Contract requirement

Refunds require an **active** contract. Where a contract is inactive,
cancelled or suspended, no financial adjustment may be made, and the request
is handled under the contract eligibility policy instead. The per-request cap
is irrelevant in that case — contract status is checked first.

## Currency

Refunds are issued in the original invoice currency. Cross-currency refunds are
not supported and must be escalated.
