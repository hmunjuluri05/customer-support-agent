# Billing Cycles and Invoice Timing

Applies to all plans. This policy explains **when** invoices are issued. It
does not authorise any refund, credit or other adjustment.

## Monthly cycle

Invoices are issued on the first day of each calendar month for the preceding
month's usage. Payment is due 14 days after issue.

## Proration

Mid-cycle plan changes are prorated to the day. A plan upgrade is billed
immediately for the remainder of the cycle; a downgrade takes effect at the
next cycle boundary.

## Failed payments

A failed payment is retried after 3 days, then after a further 7 days. After
the second failure the contract moves to suspended status.

## Invoice copies

A customer may download any historical invoice from billing settings,
including while a contract is inactive.

## Scope of this document

This document covers invoice timing only. It confers no authority to adjust a
charge; that authority sits with the plan's own policy.
