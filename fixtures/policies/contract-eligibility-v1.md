# Contract Eligibility for Financial Adjustments

This policy applies to all plans. It is checked **before** any plan-specific
refund limit.

## Active contract required

Refunds, credits and any other financial adjustment require an **active**
contract. Contract status is authoritative and is read from the account record,
never from what a customer states in conversation.

## Inactive, cancelled or suspended contracts

Where a contract is inactive, cancelled or suspended:

- No refund may be issued, regardless of the invoice amount or the plan's
  per-request cap.
- No credit may be applied to the account.
- The customer retains read-only access to historical invoices.
- The request is routed to a human billing specialist, who can review
  reinstatement alongside any adjustment.

This holds even where the requested amount is well within the plan's cap. A
Premium customer with an inactive contract requesting $15.00 is declined on
contract status, not on the amount.

## Reinstatement

Contract reinstatement is handled by the accounts team and is out of scope for
automated support. Requests to reinstate are escalated.

## Verification

Contract status and subscription tier are resolved from the verified account
bound to the session. A statement of tier or status made in conversation is
never treated as proof.
